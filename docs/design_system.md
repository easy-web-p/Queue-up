# Design System: Warm Dark Canteen

## 1. Brand & Palette
- Background: #16100C (Warm Deep Charcoal)
- Surface / Card: #241C16 (Warm Dark Canteen Surface)
- Surface Border: rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.15)
- Primary Accent: #FF7A1A (Vibrant Canteen Orange)
- Primary Hover: #E6680D
- Accent Amber: #F59E0B
- Success Green: #10B981
- Danger Red: #EF4444
- Text Primary: #FFFFFF
- Text Secondary: #E5E7EB
- Text Muted: #9CA3AF

## 2. Typography
- Headings: Kanit, sans-serif
- Body & Labels: IBM Plex Sans Thai, sans-serif
- Monospace (IDs & Queue Numbers): JetBrains Mono, monospace

## 3. Component Patterns
- Card Radius: rounded-2xl (16px) or rounded-3xl (24px)
- Button Padding: py-3 px-5, rounded-xl
- Badges: rounded-full, text-xs, font-bold
- Shadow: shadow-lg, shadow-orange-950/30
